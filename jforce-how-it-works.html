FAQ

Questions About
The System

Do I have to do CEOnyx™ first?

No. If you already know where you're bleeding, you can start directly with CoFoundry™. But most founders find CEOnyx™ reveals bottlenecks they didn't even know existed—and it right-sizes the CoFoundry™ engagement.

What if I just want CEOnyx™ and implement myself?

Perfect. CEOnyx™ stands alone as a complete engagement. Some founders have internal teams to implement. Others bring in CoFoundry™. We're flexible—and the roadmap is yours regardless of what you decide.

How long does CoFoundry™ last?

As long as you need it. Most $2M–$5M founders use CoFoundry™ for 12–18 months while building infrastructure, then transition to full-time hires. We document everything throughout for handoff.

Is the CEOnyx™ fee applied to CoFoundry™?

Yes. If you move from CEOnyx™ into a CoFoundry™ engagement, the diagnostic investment is included in your first month. You're not paying twice for the same work.

What does CoFoundry™ actually cost?

Three tiers: Foundation ($2,995/mo), Growth ($6,285/mo), Scale ($8,135/mo). Most $2M–$5M founders start at Build after CEOnyx™ identifies their bottlenecks. All tiers are month-to-month.

What happens when I'm ready to graduate?

We hand off everything: SOPs, process maps, decision frameworks, role profiles for each function you're bringing in-house. Your new full-time hires get a complete playbook, not a blank slate.
