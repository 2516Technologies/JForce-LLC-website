The Framework

Five Critical
Dimensions

CEOnyx™ evaluates your business across five dimensions that most founders have never formally assessed—let alone optimized.

1

Time Architecture

Where does your time actually go? Where should it go? What can only you do—and what shouldn't you be doing at all?

2

Delegation Infrastructure

Do you have the right people, roles, and handoff systems? Or are you "delegating" by dumping tasks with no context?

3

Operational Systems

What's documented? What's repeatable? What's in your head that needs to be on paper so someone else can own it?

4

Decision-Making Framework

Which decisions need you? How do you make sure the right things get decided fast—without becoming the approval bottleneck?

5

Team & Talent Gaps

What roles do you need next? What functions are missing? What's the gap between what you need now and what you'll need at $10M?
