Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions
Answered Honestly

Do I have to do CEOnyx™ first?

No. If you already know where you're bleeding, you can start directly with CoFoundry™. But most founders find CEOnyx™ reveals bottlenecks they didn't even know existed—and it right-sizes your tier selection.

Can I switch tiers?

Yes. Scale up or down with 30 days' notice. CoFoundry™ is designed to flex with where your business actually is—not lock you in at a level that no longer fits.

What if I only need one service?

We'd recommend starting with the CEOnyx™ diagnostic to confirm that. If you're certain you only need one function, we can discuss à la carte options—but the power of this model is integration. Isolated contractors don't produce the same results.

How long do clients typically stay?

Most clients are with us 12–18 months—long enough to build systems, stabilize operations, and prepare for full-time hires. We're always building toward your graduation, not your dependency.

Do you work with my existing team?

Absolutely. We integrate with your current employees and contractors. We're not replacing your team—we're coordinating it and filling the gaps.

Can I add custom services?

Yes. If you need something beyond the tiers—M&A support, board coordination, special projects—we can scope custom engagements. Start the conversation on the contact page.

Is CoFoundry™ Right for You?

Be Honest
With Yourself

CoFoundry™ is for you if:

  • You're generating $2M–$5M in revenue
  • You have 3–20 employees (or just yourself)
  • You're post-product-market-fit but pre-professional-management
  • You know you need operational support but can't afford full-time yet
  • You want to build systems, not just get tasks checked off
  • You're ready to stop being the integration layer

CoFoundry™ is NOT for you if:

  • You're pre-revenue (you need different support)
  • You have a full C-suite already (you're beyond this stage)
  • You want permanent outsourcing (we build toward handoff, not dependency)
  • You're looking for task execution only—not systems and infrastructure
