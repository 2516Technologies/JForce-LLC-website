The Origin of CEOnyx™

Born From Eight Years
of Seeing the Same Problem

Every founder she worked with had the same issue: they couldn't see clearly. Not because they weren't smart—they were almost always brilliant. But because they were too close to the business to see where the real time and money were going.

They had vague feelings: "I'm overwhelmed." "I'm the bottleneck." "I can't afford to hire yet." But they couldn't name exactly where the problem was—so they couldn't fix it.

CEOnyx™ was built to solve exactly that: to give founders the clarity they couldn't generate on their own, and to make that clarity actionable in 4 weeks—not 4 months.

CEO Operating System. Four weeks. One clear roadmap. That's CEOnyx™.

"I kept watching incredible founders drown in work that shouldn't have been theirs. Not because they didn't care—because they didn't have a clear picture of what was actually broken. CEOnyx™ gives them that picture."

— Jamie, Founder & CEO, J Force LLC
Our Approach

Temporary Team.
Permanent Systems.

1

Diagnosis Before Prescription

We don't assume. CEOnyx™ is the diagnosis. CoFoundry™ is the prescription. We never recommend more support than you actually need.

2

Coordination, Not Chaos

3

Build Toward Handoff

What We Stand For

Our Values

Co

Collaboration

We're on your team—not in your way. We don't compete with your existing staff, and we don't silo ourselves from your business. We integrate, we coordinate, and we make the whole thing work better together.

Ex

Excellence

We don't build systems halfway. We document processes completely. We design handoffs that actually work. The standard we hold ourselves to is: would this be usable without us in the room? If no, we haven't finished yet.

Cl

Clarity & Support
